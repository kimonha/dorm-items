# 물품 이미지 생성 기록

도구: built-in image_gen (imagegen 스킬). 원본 PNG와 웹 표시용 JPEG를 static/images/에 보관합니다. JPEG는 900px, 품질 85의 전송용 사본입니다.

생성형 이미지로 만든 물품별 예시 사진이며, 실제 보유 물품을 촬영한 사진은 아닙니다.

## vacuum

저장: static/images/vacuum.png (원본), static/images/vacuum.jpg (웹용)

프롬프트:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: dormitory shared-item card photograph. Primary request: a realistic studio product photograph of a standard compact corded canister vacuum cleaner, with a visible flexible hose, long metal wand and floor brush head, three-quarter angle. Scene/backdrop: pale sage green seamless studio background and surface. Composition: entire vacuum, hose, wand and brush fully visible centered with generous margins, landscape 3:2 composition, occupying the middle 70 percent, no cropping. Lighting: soft natural studio light, gentle shadow. Materials: light gray plastic, forest green accents. Constraints: only the one household vacuum; no people, no broom, no text, no logos, no watermark.
